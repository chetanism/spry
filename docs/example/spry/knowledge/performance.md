# Performance

## Rules

- **PERF-1** From scanning a library card or book to the desk showing it: under 1 second.
- **PERF-2** The desk works on Riverside's computer: Windows 10, 8 GB memory, Chrome.

## Why

- A queue forms at the desk on Saturday mornings; a slow scan holds everyone up.

## How it is checked

- PERF-1 — an integration test times the scan endpoints against seeded data of 20,000 loans.
- PERF-2 — manual, once per milestone, on the real computer.
