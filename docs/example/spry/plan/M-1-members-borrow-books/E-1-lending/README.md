# E-1 · Lending

## Why

- Lending is the desk's main job, and today it is all on paper.

## Who it is for

- **Librarian** — lends books and records returns quickly, without searching the register.
- **Member** — leaves with the book and knows when it is due.

## Done when

- Every loan and return at Riverside goes through the desk.
- A librarian can say who has any book in one search.

## In scope

- Lending a book at the desk.
- Recording a return.
- Limits on how many books a member may have.

## Out of scope

- Reserving a book that is on loan — later.
- Renewing a loan — later.
