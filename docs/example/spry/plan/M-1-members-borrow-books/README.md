# M-1 · Members can borrow books

## Why

- Riverside Reading Room lends about 400 books a week from a paper register.
- Librarians lose 10–15 minutes a day finding who has a book; overdue books are noticed weeks after their due date.

## Done when

- A librarian lends a book at the desk in under a minute, with no paper.
- A librarian records a return, and the book is available again straight away.
- The desk shows every overdue book and who has it.
- Riverside stops using the paper register.

## In scope

- Lending and returns at the desk.
- Member accounts, created by a librarian.
- The list of overdue books.

## Out of scope

- Members seeing their own loans online — M-2.
- Fines for overdue books.
- More than one library.

## Constraints

- Must run on the desk computer Riverside already has (Windows 10, Chrome).
- Card numbers are personal data — [security](../../knowledge/security.md).
